1. Voucher Kya Hai?

Simple Definition:

Voucher Tally me kisi business transaction ko record karne ka method hai. Business me jo financial transaction hota hai, usko appropriate Voucher ke through Tally me enter kiya jata hai.

Example:

Business ne ₹5,000 rent pay kiya.

Ye ek Payment transaction hai, isliye isko Payment Voucher me record kiya jayega.

Transaction → Voucher → Tally Record
Business Transaction
→
Voucher
→
Accounting Record

2. Important Voucher Types

Voucher Basic Use
Contra Cash aur Bank ke beech transactions ke liye.
Payment Business se payment jane par.
Receipt Business ko payment receive hone par.
Journal Adjustment aur accounting entries ke liye.
Purchase Goods purchase karne ke liye.
Sales Goods sell karne ke liye.
Debit Note Purchase return ya related adjustment ke liye commonly use hota hai.
Credit Note Sales return ya related adjustment ke liye commonly use hota hai.

3. Contra Voucher – Practical Entry

Contra ka Simple Meaning:

Contra Voucher ka use mainly Cash aur Bank ke beech money transfer jaise transactions ko record karne ke liye hota hai.

Practical Example 1 – Cash Bank me Deposit

Business ne Cash se ₹10,000 SBI Bank me deposit kiye.

Amount: ₹10,000

Entry Samjhein

Debit: SBI Bank – ₹10,000

Bank balance increase hua.
Credit: Cash – ₹10,000

Cash balance decrease hua.

Tally me Steps

1. Voucher screen open karein.
2. Contra Voucher select karein.
3. Bank account select karein.
4. Amount ₹10,000 enter karein.
5. Cash account select karein.
6. Amount ₹10,000 enter karein.
7. Entry check karke save karein.
Contra Entry – Learning Visual
Contra Voucher
Bank: SBI Bank — ₹10,000
Cash: ₹10,000
Narration: Cash deposited into bank

Practical Example 2 – Bank se Cash Withdraw

Bank se ₹5,000 cash withdraw kiya.

Debit: Cash ₹5,000
Credit: SBI Bank ₹5,000

4. Payment Voucher – Practical Entries

Payment ka Simple Meaning:

Jab business ki taraf se kisi expense, supplier ya other purpose ke liye payment ki jati hai, to Payment Voucher use kiya jata hai.

Practical Example 1 – Rent Payment

Shop ka rent ₹5,000 cash me pay kiya.

Amount: ₹5,000
Debit: Rent ₹5,000

Business ka expense increase hua.
Credit: Cash ₹5,000

Cash decrease hua.

Tally Steps

1. Payment Voucher open karein.
2. Particular me Rent Ledger select karein.
3. Amount ₹5,000 enter karein.
4. Cash Ledger select karein.
5. Narration likhein.
6. Entry save karein.
Payment Entry – Rent
Payment Voucher
Expense: Rent — ₹5,000
Paid From: Cash — ₹5,000
Narration: Rent paid in cash

Practical Example 2 – Salary Payment

Employees ko ₹15,000 salary cash me pay ki.

Debit: Salary ₹15,000
Credit: Cash ₹15,000

Practical Example 3 – Supplier ko Payment

ABC Traders ko ₹8,000 bank se pay kiye.

Debit: ABC Traders ₹8,000
Credit: SBI Bank ₹8,000

5. Receipt Voucher – Practical Entries

Receipt ka Simple Meaning:

Jab business ko kisi customer, debtor ya other source se money receive hoti hai, to Receipt Voucher use kiya jata hai.

Practical Example 1 – Customer se Cash Receive

Rahul Traders se ₹8,000 cash receive hue.

Amount: ₹8,000
Debit: Cash ₹8,000

Cash increase hua.
Credit: Rahul Traders ₹8,000

Customer ka outstanding amount decrease hua.

Tally Steps

1. Receipt Voucher open karein.
2. Cash/Bank account select karein.
3. Amount ₹8,000 enter karein.
4. Rahul Traders Ledger select karein.
5. Narration add karein.
6. Entry save karein.
Receipt Entry – Learning Visual
Receipt Voucher
Received In: Cash — ₹8,000
From: Rahul Traders — ₹8,000
Narration: Amount received from customer

6. Purchase Voucher – Practical Entries

Purchase ka Simple Meaning:

Jab business resale ya business purpose ke liye goods purchase karta hai, to Purchase Voucher ka use kiya jata hai.

Practical Example 1 – Cash Purchase

Business ne ₹20,000 ke goods cash me purchase kiye.

Amount: ₹20,000
Debit: Purchase ₹20,000
Credit: Cash ₹20,000

Steps

1. Purchase Voucher open karein.
2. Supplier/details ke according required party select karein.
3. Purchase item enter karein.
4. Quantity/Rate/Amount enter karein, agar inventory maintain kar rahe hain.
5. Appropriate purchase details complete karein.
6. Entry save karein.

Practical Example 2 – Credit Purchase

ABC Traders se ₹25,000 ke goods credit par purchase kiye.

Debit: Purchase ₹25,000
Credit: ABC Traders ₹25,000
Credit purchase me supplier ka Ledger use hota hai. GST aur inventory ke detailed purchase examples Lesson 4 me karenge.

7. Sales Voucher – Practical Entries

Sales ka Simple Meaning:

Jab business apne goods customer ko sell karta hai, to Sales Voucher ka use kiya jata hai.

Practical Example 1 – Cash Sale

Business ne ₹30,000 ke goods cash me sell kiye.

Amount: ₹30,000
Debit: Cash ₹30,000
Credit: Sales ₹30,000

Practical Example 2 – Credit Sale

Rahul Traders ko ₹18,000 ke goods credit par sell kiye.

Debit: Rahul Traders ₹18,000
Credit: Sales ₹18,000

Basic Steps

1. Sales Voucher open karein.
2. Customer/party select karein.
3. Sales item/details enter karein.
4. Quantity/Rate/Amount enter karein, agar inventory maintain kar rahe hain.
5. Required details check karein.
6. Entry save karein.

8. Journal Voucher – Practical Entries

Journal ka Simple Meaning:

Journal Voucher ka use generally adjustment aur aisi accounting entries ke liye hota hai jo normal Payment, Receipt, Contra, Purchase ya Sales transaction se directly cover nahi hoti.

Practical Example 1 – Outstanding Salary

Month end par ₹10,000 salary due hai, lekin abhi payment nahi ki gayi.

Debit: Salary ₹10,000
Credit: Salary Payable ₹10,000
Is example ke liye "Salary Payable" naam ka suitable liability Ledger business ki requirement ke according create kiya ja sakta hai.

Practical Example 2 – Depreciation

Computer par ₹2,000 depreciation record karna hai.

Debit: Depreciation ₹2,000
Credit: Computer / Accumulated Depreciation related account ₹2,000
Depreciation ki exact accounting treatment business ki accounting policy aur applicable accounting requirements ke according maintain ki ja sakti hai.

9. Debit Note – Purchase Return Practical

Debit Note ka Simple Meaning:

Debit Note ka use kai accounting situations me hota hai. Purchase return ke case me supplier ko goods return karne par commonly Debit Note use kiya ja sakta hai.

Practical Example

ABC Traders se ₹5,000 ke goods purchase kiye the. Goods me problem hone ke karan ₹2,000 ke goods supplier ko return kar diye.

Purchase Return Amount: ₹2,000
Debit: ABC Traders ₹2,000
Credit: Purchase Return ₹2,000

Basic Steps

1. Debit Note Voucher open karein.
2. Supplier ABC Traders select karein.
3. Return hone wale goods/details enter karein.
4. Amount ₹2,000 enter karein.
5. Reason/narration add karein.
6. Entry save karein.

10. Credit Note – Sales Return Practical

Credit Note ka Simple Meaning:

Credit Note ka use kai accounting situations me hota hai. Sales return ke case me customer goods return karta hai to commonly Credit Note use kiya ja sakta hai.

Practical Example

Rahul Traders ko ₹10,000 ke goods sell kiye the. Customer ne ₹3,000 ke goods return kar diye.

Sales Return Amount: ₹3,000
Debit: Sales Return ₹3,000
Credit: Rahul Traders ₹3,000

Basic Steps

1. Credit Note Voucher open karein.
2. Customer Rahul Traders select karein.
3. Return goods/details enter karein.
4. Amount ₹3,000 enter karein.
5. Narration add karein.
6. Entry save karein.

14. Important Points

  • Har transaction ke liye appropriate Voucher select karna important hai.
  • Payment ka matlab business se money outflow hona.
  • Receipt ka matlab business ko money receive hona.
  • Contra mainly Cash aur Bank ke beech transfer ke liye use hota hai.
  • Purchase Voucher goods purchase ke liye use hota hai.
  • Sales Voucher goods sale ke liye use hota hai.
  • Journal mainly adjustment type accounting entries ke liye use hota hai.
  • Purchase return ke liye commonly Debit Note use hota hai.
  • Sales return ke liye commonly Credit Note use hota hai.
  • Entry save karne se pehle date, Ledger aur amount carefully check karein.
