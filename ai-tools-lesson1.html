🤖 AI Kya Hai?

AI ka full form Artificial Intelligence hai. AI ek aisi technology hai jo computer systems ko information samajhne, patterns identify karne aur different tasks mein help karne ki ability deti hai.

Aaj AI ki madad se hum text create kar sakte hain, questions samajh sakte hain, images generate kar sakte hain, videos create kar sakte hain aur bahut se creative kaam kar sakte hain.

Simple Example:

Aap AI ko ek clear instruction dete ho aur AI us instruction ke according output generate karne ki koshish karta hai.

✍️ Prompt Kya Hota Hai?

Prompt ek instruction ya description hota hai jo hum AI ko dete hain. AI image banate samay prompt mein hum batate hain ki image mein person, clothes, location, lighting, camera style aur mood kaisa hona chahiye.

Person + Outfit + Location + Pose + Lighting + Camera + Mood + Details

Example:

Create an ultra-realistic cinematic portrait of a person wearing a black jacket, standing on a city street at night, dramatic lighting, DSLR photography, realistic skin texture.

📸 Apni Photo Ke Saath AI Photo Kaise Banaye?

Agar AI image tool mein reference image upload karne ka option hai, to aap apni photo upload karke us photo ko reference ke roop mein use kar sakte hain.

1
Ek clear photo select karein jisme face clearly visible ho.
2
AI image generation tool mein apni photo upload karein.
3
Neeche diye gaye kisi bhi prompt ko copy karein.
4
Prompt ko AI tool mein paste karein.
5
Image generate karein aur result check karein.
Important: AI tools face ko har baar 100% same nahi rakhte. Clear reference photo aur detailed identity instructions consistency ko improve kar sakte hain.

🔒 Face Same Rakhne Ke Liye Important Instruction

In prompts mein identity preserve karne ke liye instruction already include ki gayi hai.

Use the uploaded photo as the identity reference. Preserve the person's facial identity, facial structure, natural facial features and skin tone. Keep the same person recognizable. Do not replace the face with another person. Keep the result realistic and natural.

🎯 Prompt Ko Aur Better Kaise Banaye?

Agar first result perfect na aaye to poora prompt change karne ki zarurat nahi hai. Sirf required part ko modify karein.

Agar kya change karna hai Prompt mein kya change karein
Kapde Outfit description change karein
Background Location change karein
Light Lighting description change karein
Pose Standing, walking, sitting etc. likhein
Photo Style Film, DSLR, editorial, cinematic etc.
Color Warm, cool, black and white etc.

📸 Apni Photo Ke Liye Best Reference Image Tips

  • Face clearly visible hona chahiye.
  • Bahut dark photo avoid karein.
  • Face par unnecessary objects na ho.
  • High-quality photo use karna better hota hai.
  • Ek simple clear photo se starting kar sakte hain.
  • Agar tool multiple reference photos support karta hai to instructions ke according use karein.

⚠️ AI Photo Use Karte Samay Safety

AI mein kisi aur person ki photo bina permission ke upload karke unke naam ya identity ka misuse nahi karna chahiye.
  • Apni photo ya permission wali photo use karein.
  • Personal documents ki photo AI tools mein upload na karein.
  • Private information wali image upload karne se pehle carefully check karein.
  • AI generated image ko real photograph batakar misleading use na karein.
