2️⃣ Step 1 — Customer Order Karta Hai

Customer product listing dekhta hai aur agar product purchase karna chahta hai, to quantity select karke order place karta hai.

Practice Example:

Product: USB Mouse
Quantity: 2
Selling Price: ₹249 per piece

Product value:
₹249 × 2 = ₹498

Order place hone ke baad seller ko order details mil sakti hain, jaise product, quantity aur delivery information.

Customer ki personal information ko unnecessarily share nahi karna chahiye. Business ko platform ke privacy aur data-handling rules follow karne chahiye.

3️⃣ Step 2 — Order Details Check Karna

Order receive hone ke baad seller ko order information carefully check karni chahiye.

1 Product ka naam check karo.
2 Quantity check karo.
3 Available stock check karo.
4 Order status check karo.
5 Delivery information platform ke system mein verify karo.
6 Special customer instructions available hain to check karo.

4️⃣ Step 3 — Payment Kaise Work Karta Hai?

Online order mein payment different methods se ho sakta hai.

Payment Method Basic Meaning
UPI Digital payment system ke through payment
Debit/Credit Card Card ke through online payment
Net Banking Bank account ke online banking system se payment
Cash on Delivery Customer delivery ke time payment karta hai, agar option available ho
Payment ka exact settlement seller ko kab aur kis amount mein milega, ye marketplace, payment method, fees, returns aur other applicable adjustments par depend kar sakta hai.

5️⃣ Payment Ka Practical Example

Customer ne 2 USB Mouse order kiye.

Price = ₹249 × 2
Order value = ₹498

Lekin seller ko ₹498 ko automatically final earning nahi maana chahiye. Applicable marketplace fees, shipping, taxes, discounts, returns aur other charges ke baad actual settlement different ho sakta hai.

6️⃣ Step 4 — Product Packing

Order confirm hone ke baad product ko safe packing ke liye prepare kiya jata hai.

Basic Packing Process

1 Correct product nikalo.
2 Quantity dobara check karo.
3 Product ko damage ke liye check karo.
4 Suitable packing material use karo.
5 Product ko properly pack karo.
6 Required shipping label/document platform ke process ke according attach karo.
7 Final package check karo.

8️⃣ Step 5 — Shipping Kya Hai?

Shipping ka simple meaning hai product ko seller ke location se customer ke delivery address tak transport karna.

Basic Flow:

Seller → Pickup → Sorting → Transport → Local Delivery → Customer

Marketplace ya seller ke shipping setup ke according pickup aur delivery process change ho sakta hai.

🔟 Step 7 — Delivery

Delivery ke stage par product customer tak pahunchta hai.

Seller ke liye important hai ki:

  • Product correctly packed ho.
  • Correct product dispatch hua ho.
  • Shipping label/process correct ho.
  • Order status properly update ho.
  • Customer ke questions ka appropriate response diya jaye.

1️⃣1️⃣ Order Cancellation

Kabhi-kabhi customer order cancel kar sakta hai ya seller ko kisi reason se order fulfil karne mein problem ho sakti hai.

Cancellation ka exact process marketplace ki policy par depend karta hai. Seller ko cancellation ko ignore nahi karna chahiye.

Example:

Customer ne USB Mouse order kiya, lekin dispatch se pehle customer ne order cancel kar diya. Seller ko platform ke order-management system mein current status aur available cancellation option ke according action lena hoga.

1️⃣2️⃣ Return Kya Hota Hai?

Return ka meaning hai customer kisi applicable reason ke according product seller ko wapas bhejta hai.

Return ke reasons marketplace policy aur product category ke according different ho sakte hain.

Possible Reasons

  • Product damaged receive hona
  • Wrong product receive hona
  • Product description/specification se mismatch
  • Customer ki applicable return request
  • Platform policy ke according other eligible reasons
Har product automatically returnable nahi hota. Return rules product category aur platform policy ke according check karne chahiye.

1️⃣3️⃣ Refund Kya Hota Hai?

Refund ka meaning hai eligible cancelled/returned order ke case mein customer ko applicable amount wapas milna.

Example:

Customer ne eligible order cancel/return kiya.

Platform ki policy ke according refund process start ho sakta hai.

Refund amount aur timing payment method aur platform rules ke according different ho sakti hai.

1️⃣4️⃣ Customer Service

E-Commerce business mein product sell karna hi enough nahi hai. Customer ke questions aur problems ko properly handle karna bhi important hai.

Customer ke common questions:

  • Order kab deliver hoga?
  • Product available hai?
  • Return available hai?
  • Product ka size/colour kya hai?
  • Order cancel kaise karein?
  • Product damaged receive hua to kya karein?

Good Customer Service Ke Rules

1 Customer ko politely respond karein.
2 Wrong information na dein.
3 Platform ki policy ke according solution dein.
4 Customer ko unnecessary personal information share karne ko na kahen.

1️⃣6️⃣ Basic Profit Calculation

Ab ek complete simple example dekhte hain.

Item Amount
Selling Price ₹499
