2. Group Kya Hai?

Simple Definition:

Group ek category hoti hai jisme same nature ke related Ledgers ko arrange kiya jata hai.

Tally me Group ka main purpose accounts ko properly classify karna hai. Isse business ke accounts aur reports ko samajhna easy hota hai.

Example:

Agar business ke paas Rent, Salary aur Electricity jaise expenses hain, to inke Ledgers ko appropriate expense group ke andar maintain kiya ja sakta hai.

→

3. Ledger Kya Hai?

Simple Definition:

Ledger kisi particular account ka detailed record hota hai jisme us account se related transactions maintain kiye jate hain.

Business me jis particular account ka record maintain karna hota hai, uske liye Ledger create kiya jata hai.

Example:

Agar business har month ₹5,000 rent pay karta hai, to Rent naam ka Ledger banaya jayega.

Us Ledger me Rent se related transactions record honge.

Ledger ka Easy Visual

Date: 05-04-2026
Particular: Cash
Amount: ₹5,000

Ye sirf learning ke liye simplified visual hai.

4. Group aur Ledger Me Difference

Group Ledger
Group ek category hoti hai. Ledger ek particular account hota hai.
Related Ledgers ko classify karta hai. Actual transactions ka record maintain karta hai.
Example: Indirect Expenses Example: Rent
Group ke andar multiple Ledgers ho sakte hain. Ek Ledger ek particular account ko represent karta hai.
Easy Formula:

Group = Category

Ledger = Particular Account

5. Important Groups aur Unka Use

Tally me Groups ki exact number business ke according change ho sakti hai. Hum yahan accounting me commonly used Groups ko samajh rahe hain.

Group Simple Meaning / Use
Capital Account Owner ke business me investment/capital se related accounts.
Current Assets Business ke current assets se related accounts.
Current Liabilities Business ki short-term liabilities.
Direct Expenses Goods/product ki direct cost se related expenses.
Indirect Expenses Business ke general expenses.
Direct Incomes Business ki main activity se directly related income.
Indirect Incomes Main business activity ke alawa other income.
Fixed Assets Long-term business assets.
Investments Business ke investment related accounts.
Loans (Liability) Business ke loans ko maintain karne ke liye.
Purchase Accounts Purchase related Ledgers ke liye.
Sales Accounts Sales related Ledgers ke liye.
Sundry Creditors Suppliers jinhe business ko payment deni hoti hai.
Sundry Debtors Customers jinse business ko payment leni hoti hai.
Bank Accounts Bank accounts ko maintain karne ke liye.
Cash-in-Hand Cash account ke liye.
Duties & Taxes GST aur applicable taxes ke accounts ke liye.
Stock-in-Hand Stock/inventory se related accounts ke liye.

21. Important Points Yaad Rakhein

  • Group ek category hoti hai.
  • Ledger ek particular account ka record hota hai.
  • Har Ledger ko correct Group me rakhna important hai.
  • Customer ke liye appropriate Sundry Debtors classification use hoti hai.
  • Supplier ke liye appropriate Sundry Creditors classification use hoti hai.
  • Cash aur Bank ke Ledgers alag maintain kiye jate hain.
  • Purchase aur Sales ke Ledgers alag hote hain.
  • Rent aur Salary jaise general business expenses ko appropriate expense group me maintain kiya jata hai.
  • Fixed assets ke liye Fixed Assets group use hota hai.
  • GST Ledgers ko Duties & Taxes classification me maintain kiya jata hai.
