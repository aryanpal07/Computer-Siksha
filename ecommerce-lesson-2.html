📚 Lesson 2: Product Listing + Online Store Practical

Lesson 1 mein humne E-Commerce, marketplace, product research, target customer aur basic profit calculation samjha tha.

Ab hum next important step seekhenge: product ko online sell karne ke liye listing ke roop mein kaise prepare kiya jata hai.

Is lesson ka goal:

Is lesson ke end tak student samajh paayega ki ek product listing mein kaun-kaun si information hoti hai, product title kaise prepare karte hain, description kaise likhte hain, photos kaise select karte hain, price aur stock kaise manage karte hain aur ek complete sample listing kaise banate hain.

1️⃣ Product Listing Kya Hai?

Product listing wo online page ya information set hota hai jisme customer ko kisi product ke baare mein important information dikhayi jati hai.

Ek normal product listing mein generally ye information ho sakti hai:

  • Product Name
  • Product Photos
  • Product Title
  • Product Description
  • Price
  • Stock
  • Category
  • Product Features
  • Size/Colour/Variant
  • Shipping information
  • Return information

4 Features

5 Size aur Weight

6 Price

4️⃣ Step 2 — Product Title Kaise Likhein?

Product title customer ko quickly batata hai ki product kya hai. Isliye title clear aur useful hona chahiye.

❌ Weak Title

Best Mouse Amazing Product

Is title se customer ko product ki proper information nahi milti.

✅ Better Practice Title

USB Optical Mouse for Computer & Laptop - Wired Black Mouse

Title Banane Ka Simple Formula

Product Type + Main Feature + Compatibility + Important Detail

Example:

USB Mouse + Optical + Computer/Laptop + Wired
Title mein fake claims, misleading words ya aisi specification nahi likhni chahiye jo actual product mein available nahi hai.

8️⃣ Step 6 — Product Category Select Karna

Category ka purpose product ko correct section mein place karna hai.

Example:

Home → Computers & Accessories → Computer Accessories → Mouse

Exact category names marketplace ke according different ho sakte hain. Isliye seller ko available category options mein se product ke liye suitable category select karni chahiye.

Galat category select karne se product ko customer ke liye find karna difficult ho sakta hai aur marketplace rules ke according listing issue bhi aa sakta hai.

9️⃣ Step 7 — Product Price Set Karna

Price decide karte waqt sirf competitor ka price copy nahi karna chahiye. Pehle apni total cost samajhni chahiye.

Practice Example

Item Amount
Product Cost ₹150
Packing Cost ₹10
Other Applicable Cost ₹20
Total Example Cost ₹180
Example Selling Price ₹249

Simple difference:

₹249 − ₹180 = ₹69

₹69 ko automatically final net profit nahi maana ja sakta, kyunki actual business mein aur applicable fees/taxes/costs ho sakte hain.

🔟 Step 8 — Stock Kya Hota Hai?

Stock ka meaning hai ki seller ke paas kisi product ki kitni quantity available hai.

Example:

USB Mouse = 25 pieces available

Agar 3 pieces sell ho gaye:

25 − 3 = 22 pieces remaining

Stock ko accurately maintain karna important hai. Agar actual stock available nahi hai aur listing mein available dikh raha hai, to order handling problem ho sakti hai.

1️⃣3️⃣ Online Store Kya Hota Hai?

Online store ek digital shop ki tarah hota hai jahan customers products ya services ko online dekh aur purchase kar sakte hain.

Store do tarike se samjha ja sakta hai:

Type Basic Meaning
Marketplace Store Existing marketplace ke andar seller ka store
Own Website Store Apni website par products sell karna

Marketplace mein platform ka existing system mil sakta hai, jabki own website mein seller ko website, payment, shipping, customer support aur other systems ko manage karna pad sakta hai.

8 Kya koi misleading information to nahi hai?

1️⃣6️⃣ Beginners Ki Common Mistakes

  • Product ki wrong information likhna.
  • Internet se kisi aur seller ki photos bina permission use karna.
  • Fake claims karna.
  • Actual product se different photo dikhana.
  • Product cost calculate kiye bina price decide karna.
  • Stock update na karna.
  • Wrong category select karna.
  • Description bahut confusing banana.
  • Customer ke questions ka response ignore karna.
