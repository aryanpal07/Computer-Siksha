1️⃣ GST kya hota hai?

GST ka full form Goods and Services Tax hai.

GST ek indirect tax system hai jo goods aur services ki supply par applicable hota hai.

Simple Example:
Agar kisi product ki taxable value ₹10,000 hai aur applicable GST rate 18% hai, to GST ₹1,800 hoga.

18% GST ko generally do parts mein divide kiya ja sakta hai jab transaction same state ke andar ho:

  • CGST = 9%
  • SGST = 9%

Inter-state transaction mein generally IGST apply hota hai.

2️⃣ CGST, SGST aur IGST

Tax Meaning Basic Use
CGST Central Goods and Services Tax Same-state supply mein central share
SGST State Goods and Services Tax Same-state supply mein state share
IGST Integrated Goods and Services Tax Inter-state supply
Important: Actual GST treatment transaction ki nature, place of supply, registration status aur applicable GST rules par depend karta hai. Website par diye examples learning purpose ke liye hain.

5️⃣ Inventory kya hoti hai?

Inventory ka matlab business mein available goods/products ka record maintain karna.

Example:

  • Mobile
  • Keyboard
  • Mouse
  • Printer
  • Pen Drive

Agar shop ke paas 50 mouse hain aur 5 mouse sell ho gaye, to stock balance 45 mouse ho jayega.

Inventory ka main purpose:
Business ke paas kitna stock hai, kitna purchase hua aur kitna sell hua — iska record maintain karna.

6️⃣ Inventory ke important terms

Stock Category Common characteristics ke basis par stock ko classify karna
Stock Item Actual product/item jiska stock maintain hota hai
Unit Product ki measurement unit jaise Nos, Kg, Box
Godown Stock rakhne ki location

1️⃣2️⃣ Practical Purchase Entry with GST

Ab sabse important practical example karte hain.

Transaction:

ABC Traders se 10 USB Mouse credit par purchase kiye.
Rate = ₹500 per Mouse
Taxable Value = ₹5,000
Practice GST Rate = 18%
CGST = ₹450
SGST = ₹450
Total = ₹5,900

Entry ka basic calculation

Particular Amount
10 × ₹500 ₹5,000
CGST 9% ₹450
SGST 9% ₹450
Total ₹5,900

Tally mein basic process

  1. Purchase Voucher open karo.
  2. Supplier invoice details enter karo.
  3. Party A/C select karo - ABC Traders.
  4. Purchase ledger select karo.
  5. Stock Item - USB Mouse select karo.
  6. Quantity = 10 Nos.
  7. Rate = ₹500.
  8. Applicable GST details check karo.
  9. CGST aur SGST calculate/check karo.
  10. Voucher save karo.
PURCHASE VOUCHER Party A/C : ABC Traders Item : USB Mouse Quantity : 10 Nos Rate : ₹500 Taxable Value : ₹5,000 CGST 9% : ₹450 SGST 9% : ₹450 Invoice Total : ₹5,900 Save

1️⃣6️⃣ Inter-State Purchase/Sales mein IGST

Agar transaction different states ke beech hai, to applicable conditions ke according IGST use ho sakta hai.

Example:
Gujarat ki company ne Maharashtra ke registered customer ko taxable value ₹10,000 ka product sell kiya.

Practice GST Rate = 18%
IGST = ₹1,800
Invoice Total = ₹11,800

Is example mein CGST + SGST ke badle IGST treatment use kiya jayega, subject to actual GST rules.

SALES Party : Maharashtra Customer Taxable Value: ₹10,000 IGST 18% : ₹1,800 Total : ₹11,800
