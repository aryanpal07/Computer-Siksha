9️⃣ Transaction 3 - Credit Purchase

ABC Traders se 10 USB Mouse ₹500 per piece ke rate se purchase kiye.

Taxable value:

10 × ₹500 = ₹5,000

Practice GST 18% hone par:

  • CGST 9% = ₹450
  • SGST 9% = ₹450
  • Total = ₹5,900
Purchase Voucher Party : ABC Traders Item : USB Mouse Qty : 10 Nos Rate : ₹500 Taxable Value : ₹5,000 CGST : ₹450 SGST : ₹450 Total : ₹5,900 Save

2️⃣2️⃣ Stock Summary Check Karna

Ab purchase aur sales ke baad Stock Summary check karo.

Basic formula:

Closing Stock = Opening Stock + Purchase - Sales + Sales Return - Purchase Return

Mouse ka example

Details Quantity
Opening 20
Purchase 10
Sales 4
Purchase Return 2
Sales Return 1
Closing 25

Calculation: 20 + 10 - 4 - 2 + 1 = 25 Nos

2️⃣7️⃣ Trial Balance

Trial Balance mein ledgers ke debit aur credit balances ka summary view milta hai.

Accounting system mein properly recorded transactions ke case mein total debit aur total credit balances normally equal hone chahiye.

TRIAL BALANCE Cash SBI Bank Computer Purchase Sales Expenses Capital Creditors Debtors GST Ledgers ----------------------------- Total Debit = Total Credit -----------------------------
Agar Trial Balance expected way mein balance nahi ho raha, to ledger selection, opening balance aur voucher entries ko carefully check karo.
